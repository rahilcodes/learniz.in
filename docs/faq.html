Learniz is priced on a transparent, affordable annual per-student or per-school tier. There are zero hidden implementation charges, server fees or per-feature add-ons. During our Founding Schools Programme, ten selected schools receive a 100% free 30-day pilot and locked founding rates.

The free pilot gives your school unrestricted access to the interactive experiments, the IntelliQ question engine, the complete LMS and exam builder, and student performance analytics for all registered teachers and students.

At the end of the 30 days, your school leadership can choose to continue on our locked founding-tier rate, or simply conclude the pilot. There is zero automatic billing, credit-card requirement or lock-in contract.

Learniz is natively mapped to CBSE, ICSE and Indian State Board standards for Classes 6 through 12 across Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics. All learning objectives conform to the National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE 2023).
